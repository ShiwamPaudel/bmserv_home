# BMServ

BMServ is an installable web app for a hospital's Biomedical Department. It keeps every piece of medical equipment in one register, by department, and makes sure the right people hear about problems until they are fixed.

- **Equipment register**: each device with its model, serial number, department, service provider and photo.
- **Models and manuals**: service and operation manuals kept once per model, open on any phone.
- **QR labels**: scan a label to see the equipment's history, or to report a problem.
- **Breakdowns**: the service provider is emailed once a day at 08:00 Nepal time until the equipment works again.
- **Hospital staff reports**: nurses scan a label and tell the Biomedical team what is wrong; the team gets a notification.
- **Warranty, AMC and CMC**: coverage dates, with PMS visits planned and reminded automatically.
- **Moves**: where equipment is now, where it belongs, and when it should come back.
- **Home overview**: what is down, what is due, and how the department is doing at a glance.
- **Nepali or English dates**, WhatsApp buttons, and it works as an app on phones, tablets and computers.
